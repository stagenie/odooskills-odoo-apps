Treasury — Cash Registers & Safes

Every euro coming into or out of your cash registers is tracked, closed and justified — no Excel, no notebook.

The cash register is accounting's blind spot: cash payments are recorded in Odoo, but nobody knows how much is really in the drawer, who made the last withdrawal, or why €20 is missing at the end of the week. Checks are done in a notebook or a spreadsheet — never reconciled, never locked.

What it brings

  • 💶 Cash registers with real-time balance — every Odoo payment recorded on a cash journal automatically creates the mirror cash operation.
  • 🔗 Chained closings — each closing starts from the validated balance of the previous one: difference displayed, locked once validated, signable PDF report.
  • 🔒 Safes — designated custodians, their own operation history, a balance kept separate from the cash registers.
  • 🔁 Controlled transfers — register ↔ register, register ↔ safe, with before/after balance simulation and a 3-level control (block, warning, free).
  • 🧾 Configurable operation categories — sales, expenses, transfers… each linked to its journal entry.
  • 👥 Authorized users per register — a cashier sees only their own registers; menus are hidden from other users.

Why adopt it

  • Physical cash management does not exist in standard Odoo — not even in Enterprise. This module provides it, for free.
  • No more differences discovered weeks later: the chained closing shows them the same day.
  • A payment linked to a validated closing can no longer be cancelled or reset to draft — the cash history is reliable.
