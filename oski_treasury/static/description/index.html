Treasury — Cash & Safe Management

A complete, self-contained treasury layer for physical cash: cash registers with a running balance, chained (sequential) closings, safes, nine transfer routes between registers and safes, configurable operation categories and accounting entries, and automatic mirroring of Odoo payments into treasury operations — all built free, on top of standard account.

Key features

  • Cash registers with a live running balance and per-register authorized users.
  • Chained closings: each closing starts from the previous validated balance, with a lock once validated.
  • Safes with responsible users and their own operation history.
  • Four transfer routes (register ↔ register, register ↔ safe, safe ↔ register, safe ↔ safe) — five more bank routes with the Bank pro module with balance controls and an optional manager override.
  • Configurable operation categories mapped to accounting entries.
  • Automatic mirror operation created when a treasury-enabled user posts a payment on a cash journal.

Design notes

Payments and treasury access. A payment posted by a user who has no treasury access group creates no mirror treasury operation. The register balance and the accounting journal balance stay in sync only for payments recorded by users who belong to a treasury group; grant the relevant group before their payments should feed the cash register / safe balances.

Closing locks and mirror cleanup. Cancelling or resetting a payment to draft always enforces the closing locks, for every user: a payment attached to a validated or confirmed closing cannot be cancelled or reset. The mirror-operation cleanup that follows runs under a payment-scoped sudo() — the treasury record rules (per-assignment access to registers/safes) govern who can operate the treasury directly, not a payment's own mirror maintenance.
