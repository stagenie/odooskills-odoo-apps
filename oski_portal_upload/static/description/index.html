Portal Document Upload

You ask, the customer uploads — from their quotation, their invoice, their task.

The signed purchase order, the insurance certificate, the measurement sheet: these documents arrive by email, get lost in an inbox, and nobody can say which ones are still missing. In Odoo 19, a customer can only send a file from the portal by writing a message in the discussion thread, and only on the pages that display it. Nothing tells them what is expected of them.

What it adds

  • 📥 A "Requested documents" block on the portal page: document name, details, due date, and the field to upload it.
  • 📋 Back-office tracking — requested, received, cancelled, with overdue items in red.
  • 🧷 The file joins the record as an attachment, and the discussion thread notifies the team.
  • 🌐 Quotation, invoice, purchase order, task — the block hooks into the portal discussion thread, shared by all these pages.

Why install it

  • Uploading is authorized on the record, through the portal's own mechanism — read rights or access token. A stolen request link is useless on its own.
  • Accepted extensions and maximum size are configurable. A rejected file brings the customer back to their page with the reason, never to an error page.
  • In a company, the named person is not always the one who uploads: the other contacts of the same company can do it too.
  • Open source module, LGPL-3 license — no subscription, no dependency on a business module.
