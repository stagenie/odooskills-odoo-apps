Low Stock Alert

Get warned before you run out — a threshold per product, red rows and a daily activity for the inventory manager, with no technical setup.

Odoo's replenishment rules trigger purchase orders — but they don't warn a human when a product slips below its critical threshold. The result: silent stockouts discovered too late, after a refused delivery or a blocked order line. What's missing is simply a visible signal and a daily reminder for the right person.

What it brings

  • 🎯 Custom threshold per product — a simple numeric field in the Inventory tab; setting it to 0 disables the alert.
  • 🔴 Red rows in the list — as soon as the available quantity drops below the threshold, the row is highlighted in red so you see it at a glance.
  • 🔍 Dedicated “Low stock” filter — isolate every product on alert in one click, with no custom query.
  • 📅 Automatic daily activity — a scheduled action creates a “To-Do” activity every day on each product on alert, assigned to the inventory manager.
  • ✅ Built-in duplicate prevention — as long as the previous activity is not done, no new alert is created for that product.

Why adopt it

  • Open source and self-contained — no external dependency, no third-party service subscription.
  • Zero training: the threshold lives in the Inventory tab your teams already know.
  • Alerts arrive in native Odoo activities — no extra email notification to configure.
  • Duplicate prevention keeps the noise down: one activity per product until it is handled, no daily flood.
