Partner Balance

The account statement Community is missing: every invoice, credit note and payment in order, with the balance recalculated on every line — on screen, in PDF and in Excel.

Odoo Community can tell you a customer owes $16,500. It cannot tell you how that number came about. When the customer calls to dispute it, someone has to open the invoices one by one, hunt down the payments, and re-add everything by hand — hoping to land on the same figure the customer did. The subsidiary ledger that every bookkeeper asks for in the first week simply does not exist in Community.

What it adds

  • 📄 The chronological statement — invoices, credit notes and payments in the order they happened, with the running balance recalculated on every line. The last line IS the customer's balance.
  • 🔁 Opening balance — the balance at the start date sits on a single opening line, so a quarter's statement stays correct without going back to the day the account was created.
  • 🎯 Four scopes — customer only, vendor only, both side by side, or netted into a single balance when the partner is both at once.
  • 🔍 Journal filters — include or exclude journals that have nothing to do with the conversation at hand.
  • ✂️ Exclude a single document — a disputed invoice can be taken out of the computation with one checkbox, without touching the accounting.
  • 📤 PDF and Excel — built from what is on screen, not a second computation: the export can never contradict the screen.

Why install it

  • Free, LGPL-3, no dependency on an Enterprise module.
  • The statement is scoped to the user who produced it: each user only sees the lines they just generated, and the figures stay reserved for accounting profiles.
  • Multi-company: the statement describes the books of the document's company, never those of the session reading it.
  • This is the foundation of the whole Partner Balance family — aged balance, email follow-ups, credit limit control all plug into it without recomputing anything.
