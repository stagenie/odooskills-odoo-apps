Required Lost Reason

No opportunity lost without a reason, and what each reason really costs.

In Odoo, the lost reason is optional: the wizard shows it, nobody is required to fill it in. Six months later, half of the lost pipeline has no explanation, and the question "why are we losing?" has no answer in figures. The core does count opportunities per reason — but it does not say what each one takes away.

What it adds

  • 🚫 The reason becomes required, at the model level and not just on screen — an import, a server action or a direct call are subject to the same rule.
  • ✍️ A closing note can be required: a few words of context for whoever takes over this customer a year from now.
  • 💶 Lost revenue per reason, totaled in the list, with the date of the last loss.
  • 🏢 Both requirements are set per company, in the CRM settings.

Why install it

  • The rule lives on the model: a requirement that only lived on screen would leave every other door open — import, server action, direct call.
  • The wizard shows the requirement up front instead of revealing it once the form is filled in.
  • "Too expensive" on two deals of a thousand euros and on one of two hundred thousand does not call for the same response: the module gives the amount, not just the count.
  • Open source module, LGPL-3 license — no subscription, no dependency beyond CRM.
