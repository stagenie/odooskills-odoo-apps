Knowledge Base

A hierarchical knowledge base for the whole team — unlimited articles, native rich editor, shared and private spaces, free in Odoo Community.

Odoo's Knowledge app has been reserved for the Enterprise edition since v16: on Community, internal procedures, operating instructions and team notes end up scattered across attachments, personal notes and external documents — impossible to find and never up to date. This app rebuilds a complete knowledge base for Odoo 19 Community, with no external dependency.

What it adds

  • 🌳 Unlimited article tree — organize your articles in a hierarchy with no depth limit, with ordered favorites and a trash to recover an article deleted by mistake.
  • 🗂️ Two spaces — a Workspace shared by the whole team and a personal Private space, separated from installation.
  • ✍️ Native Odoo HTML editor — headings, tables, images, checklists: the same rich editor as the rest of Odoo, with no external JavaScript.
  • 🧭 Notion-style sidebar — a dedicated client action with the tree on the left and the embedded article form on the right, to navigate without switching screens.
  • 🔎 Search — find an article by its title or content, with standard filters and groupings.
  • 🔒 Secure by default — a private article is truly private: access rights are tested, not just displayed.

Who it is for

  • SMEs on Community who want a Notion-style knowledge base without an Enterprise license.
  • Teams documenting internal procedures who want a single up-to-date source rather than scattered files.
  • Anyone who wants to try a knowledge base before investing in fine-grained permissions and public sharing (Pro edition).

What the open-source version does not do (v1, in all honesty)

No inline comments anchored in the text, no properties or custom fields, no embedded views or items, no cover image on the article, no drag-and-drop in the sidebar (moving an article is done with a button). Fine-grained permissions per article, public sharing by link, version history, article templates and PDF export are in the Pro edition.

Pair this knowledge base with the Documents — DMS module (oski_dms) to file your documents in separate spaces, and with Knowledge Base Pro (oski_knowledge_pro) for per-article permissions, public sharing and version history. See the full suite on apps.odooskills.com.
