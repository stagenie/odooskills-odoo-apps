Report Watermark

DRAFT, COPY, CANCELLED — on any report, and on every one of its pages.

An unconfirmed quotation, a cancelled invoice and a reprint come out of the printer looking exactly like the valid document. The customer cannot tell them apart, and neither can anyone at the office once the sheet is sitting on a pile. Odoo 19 offers no watermark: the only one in the codebase is used internally to mark its own demo documents, and is open to no one.

What it adds

  • 🖨️ Any QWeb report — quotation, invoice, delivery slip, manufacturing order, including your in-house reports.
  • 🎯 Conditional — one rule = one report, one condition on the document, one word. DRAFT as long as the quotation is not confirmed, CANCELLED as soon as it no longer stands.
  • 📄 Every page — the watermark is stamped on each page of the generated PDF, behind the text: page 7 is marked just like page 1.
  • 🎨 Adjustable — word, color, opacity, angle, size. Subtle by default, so the text underneath stays readable.

Real screenshots

1. The quotation, as it prints

A quotation still being drafted carries its word across the page, without hiding any amount or line. Once confirmed, it will print clean — the rule's condition decides, not the user.

2. The rules, in one place

Three rules, three reports, three conditions. They can be reordered with the mouse: when several accept the same document, the first one wins.

Why install it

  • The condition is a domain, checked on save against the report's model: no code is ever executed at print time, and a faulty rule is rejected in front of its author.
  • A print job that groups documents calling for different watermarks applies none: better to mark nothing than to mark wrongly.
  • No special right is needed to print a marked document; the configuration remains reserved for administrators.
  • Free module, LGPL-3 license — no subscription, no external dependency.
