Shop Floor

Run your work orders by touch, on a tablet or phone, without ever opening a desktop view — the Shop Floor experience in Community.

On the production line, the operator has neither a mouse nor a large screen: they have a tablet sitting on the work center, sometimes with dirty or gloved hands. Odoo Community's native MRP views are still designed for a desk — dense lists, tiny buttons, no visible timer. The result: work orders started late, untracked stoppages and quantities entered approximately at the end of the day. What is missing is a finger-sized screen, designed to be touched from the work center, not from an office chair.

What it brings

  • 🏭 Work center selection — the operator taps their work center and only sees the orders that concern them.
  • ▶️ Start, pause, finish by touch — one large button per action, with no navigation or form to fill in.
  • ⏱️ Real-time timer — the actual working time is displayed during execution, pauses included.
  • 🔢 Produced quantity entered on screen — the quantity produced is entered directly on the current order, not after the fact.
  • 📷 Component consumption by scan — each consumed component is recorded by scanning its code, without searching for the line in a list.
  • 📱 Responsive on PC, tablet and phone — the same screen adapts from the fixed work center to the mobile operator's smartphone.

Why adopt it

  • The Enterprise Shop Floor experience, in Community — with no per-user subscription.
  • Responsive even beyond the Enterprise version: fixed work center, shop tablet or the mobile operator's phone.
  • Driven by scanning, not by the mouse — designed for busy hands and a touch screen.
  • Relies only on Odoo's standard Manufacturing and Barcode modules.

Coming soon: quality control at the work center & lots/serials by scan

Two extensions will enrich these same screens: Shop Floor — Quality Control will add quality control points directly on the current order if the Quality module is installed, and Shop Floor — Barcode will add lot or serial number entry by scan when consuming components.
