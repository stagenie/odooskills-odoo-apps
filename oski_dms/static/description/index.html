Documents — DMS

Organize all your files in isolated workspaces: versioning, preview, links to business records and per-workspace access rights — right inside Odoo Community.

On Odoo Community, documents live scattered as attachments buried in records, with no filing, no version history, no control over who sees what. Finding the right contract, knowing which is the latest version, keeping one department out of another's files: none of this exists natively. Odoo Enterprise's DMS (Documents) fills that gap — this app brings it to your Community.

What it adds

  • 🗂️ Hierarchical workspaces — file your documents in a tree of workspaces (HR, Contracts, Accounting…), with a document counter and sub-workspaces.
  • 🔒 Per-workspace access rights — each workspace defines who can read, write and manage (by group). One department does not see another's documents. Real isolation, not cosmetic.
  • 🧾 Documents = real records — each document wraps an Odoo attachment (ir.attachment) and carries its metadata: workspace, tags, owner, type, size.
  • 🕓 Versioning — upload a new version of a document; the old one is kept and can be restored in one click. No more "contract_v3_final_REALLY.pdf".
  • 🔗 Links to business records — link a document to any record (invoice, project, employee) and open it in one click. "File in the DMS" an existing attachment, without duplicating it.
  • 🔎 Multi-criteria search — filter by workspace, tag, file type, owner or date; group by workspace or owner.

Who it is for

  • SMBs on Community who want Enterprise's DMS without switching editions.
  • HR, accounting and legal teams who handle sensitive documents and need isolation.
  • Anyone who wants to organize the attachments already in Odoo instead of leaving them scattered.

Goes well with

Two separate apps work alongside it: Electronic Signature (oski_sign) to have contracts signed online, and Portal Document Upload (oski_portal_upload) for documents requested from customers through their portal. They do not need this DMS, and this DMS does not need them.
