Helpdesk — Oski

Centralize every customer request as a structured ticket: nothing falls through the cracks anymore, every issue has an owner and a resolution date.

Without a dedicated tool, support requests get scattered across emails, calls and handwritten notes. Some go unanswered for days, others are handled twice by two different agents — and nobody really knows what is open, blocked or solved. The result: frustrated customers and a support team flying blind.

What it adds

  • ✅ Automatically numbered tickets — subject, priority (low to urgent), customer, team, HTML description. Every ticket has a reference number from the moment it is created.
  • 📧 Creation by email — set up an alias per team (e.g. support@yourcompany.com): every incoming email becomes a ticket with no manual action.
  • ⚖️ Workload-balanced assignment — in automatic mode, the new ticket is assigned to the agent with the fewest open tickets among the team members.
  • 📊 Kanban view by stage — see all tickets by status at a glance (New / In Progress / Solved…), with a colored blocked indicator and the assigned agent's avatar.
  • 🔍 Advanced filters and grouping — instantly find "my tickets", unassigned ones, open ones; group by team, stage, agent or priority.
  • 💬 Built-in chatter and activities — internal discussions, full change history and follow-up activities (call, email, meeting) right on each ticket.
  • 📈 Pivot and graphs — analyze how tickets are distributed by team, stage and priority without leaving the interface.

Why adopt it

  • 100% open source, no external dependency — installs on any Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise instance (only requires the base and mail modules).
  • Up and running in minutes — the stages (New, In Progress, Solved) and the numbering sequence are preconfigured at installation.
  • Extensible architecture — this module is the core of the Oski Helpdesk suite. Add Pro add-ons as your needs grow: SLA, customer portal, canned replies, satisfaction rating, advanced reports.
  • Full traceability — every change of priority, stage or assignment is recorded in the chatter; ideal for internal audits and quality monitoring.
