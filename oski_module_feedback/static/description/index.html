Module Change Requests

Let the customers of your module store tell you what is missing.

Adds a Request a change button to the page of every paid module of an OdooSkills store. Signed-in customers report a bug, ask for a change or an improvement, with screenshots. Each request lands in a Kanban pipeline (New → Under review → Planned → Delivered / Declined), and the customer follows its status and your answer from their portal.

  • Form pre-filled with the module and the Odoo version; steps to reproduce for bugs.
  • Up to 3 attachments (images, PDF), 5 MB each; daily limit per customer.
  • Internal e-mail notification and acknowledgement in the customer's language.
  • "My requests" in the customer portal, with status changes sent by e-mail.
  • Link from the 1-hour trial pages and the end-of-trial e-mail.

Requires the OdooSkills store module (oski_app_store).
