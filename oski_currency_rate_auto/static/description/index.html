Automatic Currency Rates

Rates update on their own, from the European Central Bank.

Odoo Community can keep a table of exchange rates; it cannot fill it. So every morning someone copies rates by hand — or nobody does, and conversions drift silently on invoices, purchases and reports.

What it adds

  • 🏦 A public, free source — the ECB reference rates. No API key, no account to open.
  • 🧭 Your currency is the pivot, whatever it is. The ECB publishes in euros; the module does the division. A company in dollars gets rates seen from the dollar.
  • 🔁 A single rate per currency per day — a second run corrects the day's rate instead of adding another one.
  • 😴 The scheduled action sleeps until someone asks for it — an installed module does not call the ECB every night without consent.

Real screenshots

1. One checkbox, one source, one date

The tab added to the company fits in four lines: the toggle, the source, the date of the last success, and a button for an immediate update. In case of failure, the error shows here — a message in the server log cannot be read from the interface.

2. The history fills itself

One rate per day and per currency, recorded where Odoo expects it: invoice and report conversions rely on it with no other setting.

Why install it

  • One failing company does not stop the others: the error is recorded on the company, and the update carries on.
  • Inactive currencies are left aside, and those missing from the feed skip their turn without making the rest fail.
  • Free module, LGPL-3 license — no subscription, no external dependency.
