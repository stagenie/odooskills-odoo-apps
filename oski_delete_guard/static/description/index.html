Delete Guard

Block deletion model by model, reserve it for a few groups, and keep a record of every deletion.

A deletion leaves nothing behind: the record disappears, and its chatter with it. Odoo only offers one global right per model, granted or denied once and for all in the access rights — with no possible exception, no explanatory message, and not the slightest record of what was deleted.

What it adds

  • 🛡️ Lock model by model — choose the models to protect: orders, contacts, journal entries, whatever you decide.
  • 🔑 Grant the exception to whoever you want — the designated groups keep the right to delete. No designated group means: nobody.
  • 💬 Explain the refusal — the message shown is yours ("Archive instead of deleting"), not a technical error.
  • 📓 Log without blocking — the "Allow, but log" mode leaves the door open and keeps a record: model, ID, display name, author, date.
  • 🔧 Maintenance is never blocked — installations, upgrades, uninstallations and scheduled actions pass through the guard: it protects the interface, not the server.

Real screenshots

1. One rule per model

Contacts can be deleted but every deletion is logged; tags are locked for everyone; orders can only be deleted by sales managers.

2. Your message, not a technical error

Deleting a tag is refused with the message written in the rule, administrator included.

3. What disappeared, and who did it

Each deleted contact keeps its ID, its name at the time of deletion, the author and the date: the only details nothing can rebuild afterwards.

Why adopt it

  • The name and ID are captured before the deletion: afterward, nothing can rebuild them.
  • No global setting to change: the guard sits on top of your existing access rights, without redefining them.
  • Open source module, LGPL-3 license — no subscription, no external dependency.
