Appointments — Internal Booking

Manage your appointment types, compute free slots and book in a few clicks — without leaving Odoo Community.

Odoo Community ships with no appointment booking module. The result: every consultation, session or service call is entered by hand in the calendar, with no check on staff availability. Double bookings happen, confirmation emails are forgotten, and "Attended / No-show / Cancelled" tracking simply does not exist. Every team cobbles together its own solution in a spreadsheet or an external tool.

What it adds

  • 📋 Configurable appointment types — set duration, authorized staff, hours (working schedule), minimum notice and booking horizon for each type of service.
  • ⚡ Available slots computed automatically — the module cross-checks the working schedule and existing events to offer only truly free slots, per staff member.
  • 🖱️ One-click booking from the form — a built-in wizard lets you select the customer, the slot and the staff member, then confirm the appointment without leaving Odoo.
  • 📊 Appointment status tracking — each event carries a status (Booked, Attended, Cancelled, No-show) to steer your activity and filter calendars.
  • ✉️ Automatic confirmation email — an email template is sent to the customer and the staff member for every new booking.
  • 🗓️ Native Odoo Calendar integration — each appointment becomes a standard calendar event, visible in the calendar, with configurable reminders.
