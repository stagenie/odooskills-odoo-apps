Record Audit Log

Who created, changed or deleted what — on the models you choose, without writing a line of code.

Odoo's native tracking only covers fields marked "tracking" on models that have a chatter. A price changed on a product form, a bank account rewritten, a line deleted from a technical table: none of it leaves a trace. The day you need to understand what happened, there is nothing to read.

What it adds

  • 🔍 Any model you want — pick it, choose the tracked operations (create, update, delete), and it is in place.
  • ↔️ Before and after — every change is written out in plain words: Reference : C-STU → CLI-0067.
  • 🎯 The fields that matter — limit monitoring to a list of fields, or leave it empty to track everything.
  • 🧾 The name at the time of the action — a deletion keeps the ID and the display name, the only information that cannot be recovered afterwards.
  • ⚙️ Without maintenance noise — operations run as superuser (installation, update, scheduled actions) are not logged.

Why install it

  • An answer to "who did this?" that does not depend on the chatter, and therefore works on technical models as well as business models.
  • Attachments, images and related lists are deliberately ignored: their content does not fit on a log line, and forcing them in would make the log unreadable.
  • Open source module, LGPL-3 license — no subscription, no external dependency.
