CRM Dormant Leads

Spot at a glance the opportunities your pipeline has forgotten — before they are lost for good.

In an active pipeline, opportunities pile up and some slip into the blind spot: nobody has touched them for weeks, no salesperson remembers them. Odoo's native CRM does not flag this stagnation — no warning color, no dedicated filter, no visible counter. These dormant deals are revenue within reach that the sales team simply does not follow up on.

What it adds

  • 📊 "Idle Days" column — shows in the pipeline list the number of days elapsed since each opportunity's last stage change.
  • ⚠️ Immediate visual highlighting — dormant rows stand out in orange in the list, easy to spot without reading the figures.
  • ⚙️ Threshold configurable in the CRM Settings — set the number of days yourself (14 by default) to match your real sales cycle.
  • 🔎 One-click "Idle" filter — instantly isolate every deal to follow up for an efficient review session.
  • 🏷️ Badge on the opportunity form — a discreet "Idle for X days" indicator appears right on the form as long as the deal is open.

Why install it

  • No forgotten deals — every open opportunity is monitored automatically, with no extra configuration after installation.
  • Fits your pace — the inactivity threshold reflects your sales cycle, not an imposed generic setting.
  • Zero friction for the team — the highlighting and the column are visible right in the existing views, without changing work habits.
  • Open source and lightweight — depends only on Odoo's standard CRM module, compatible with Community and Enterprise.
